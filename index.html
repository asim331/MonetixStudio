How we work

Useful first

Every page should answer a real question better than the alternatives. If we can't add something, we don't publish it.

Honest about money

Our sites earn from affiliate commissions and display ads. Affiliate links are disclosed on every page, and commissions never decide what we recommend.

No made-up content

No invented codes, fake reviews or made-up numbers. When we cite a fact, we link to where it came from.

Respect for readers

Fast pages, no pop-ups, and we never sell personal information.

For brands and networks

We work with brands through affiliate networks and display advertising. We don't bid on brand terms, run code-injecting toolbars, or send incentivized traffic.

We don't promote adult products, gambling, legal services, tobacco, vaping, cannabis, weapons, financial products or prescription and medical products.
